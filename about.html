OUR LITTLE STORY

Less clutter.
More keepers.

Shopping online can feel like a lot: endless choices, unclear descriptions, and products that look better in a photo than in real life. ShopSphere is our answer to that feeling.

We imagine a store where useful everyday objects get the spotlight. Think dependable tech accessories, small style upgrades, desk essentials, and thoughtful gifts — all presented with clear details and simple choices.

This website is a fictional student project. Product names, prices, stock counts, reviews, and promotional offers are sample data created to demonstrate a complete e-commerce interface. It is not a real retail business and does not accept real payments.

THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

Before you check out.

Example store policies for the purposes of this website project.

Shipping & delivery

For this demo, standard delivery is estimated at 3–7 business days within the Philippines. Orders worth ₱1,500 or more qualify for sample free shipping. Actual courier fees and timelines would be confirmed at checkout in a real store.

Returns & exchanges

Sample policy: request a return or exchange within 7 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. This is illustrative content only; a real store should publish its approved policy and applicable consumer rights.

Payments

This school project does not collect payment details or process transactions. A production store would use a secure payment provider and clearly display available payment methods before order confirmation.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

A few helpful answers to common shopping questions.

Still wondering about something? Send us a message using the sample contact details below.

Are the products and prices real?
No. ShopSphere is a fictional e-commerce demo for an academic project. Product details, pricing, reviews, stock, and discounts are sample data created to make the website feel realistic.
Can I place an actual order?
You can add items to the demo shopping bag and test the checkout interaction, but no order is sent to a real store and no payment is collected.
How do I find a specific item?
Visit the Shop page to search by product name or description, filter by category, set a maximum price, and sort products by price or rating.
How is shipping calculated?
The free-shipping threshold shown across the site is sample content. A real store would calculate shipping using delivery location, package size, courier rates, and current promotions.
